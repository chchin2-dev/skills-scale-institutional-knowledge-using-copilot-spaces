# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsors

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide business-level sponsorship and help ensure an initiative remains aligned with organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment and provide sponsorship for the initiative
- Make or escalate decisions that are outside the delivery team's authority
- Help resolve organizational blockers and secure support when needed

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative has clear organizational backing
- Enable timely decisions and resolution of escalated blockers

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Partner with the Project Manager on escalations, major risks, and organizational blockers
- Align with the Product Manager on intended outcomes, priority, and significant scope decisions
- Provide direction and decisions to the delivery team through the Project Manager and Product Manager

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business needs and translate them into requirements that stakeholders and delivery teams can understand and validate.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business needs, workflows, and constraints
- Help translate objectives into requirements and testable acceptance criteria
- Identify gaps, assumptions, and questions that need stakeholder decisions

### Goals
- Ensure the team shares an accurate understanding of the problem and requirements
- Reduce ambiguity and rework by making requirements clear and verifiable

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Work with the Product Manager and stakeholders to clarify needs, scope, and priorities
- Coordinate with the Project Manager to surface requirement dependencies and decisions that may affect delivery
- Answer requirement questions from Developers and QA/Testing, and help confirm that proposed tests cover agreed acceptance criteria

---

## UX/UI Designers and User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers investigate user needs and shape usable, accessible experiences that support the product goals.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and validate assumptions about user problems
- Create or maintain user flows, interaction patterns, and design artifacts
- Define usability and accessibility considerations for proposed experiences

### Goals
- Help deliver experiences that are useful and understandable to users
- Validate design decisions with user needs and evidence

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Collaborate with the Product Manager on user needs, product goals, and prioritization
- Work with Developers and the Technical Lead to assess feasibility and carry designs into implementation
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility expectations that should be validated
- Share research findings and design decisions with the Project Manager when they affect scope, dependencies, or timing

---

## Technical Leads and Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical design and help the team make consistent decisions about implementation, integration, and technical quality.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and integration decisions
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and quality attributes such as reliability and maintainability
- Support Developers with implementation direction and technical trade-offs

### Goals
- Enable a coherent, supportable technical solution
- Surface technical risks and dependencies early enough for the team to address them

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Collaborate with Developers on design, implementation, and code quality
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on technical dependencies, risks, and delivery impacts
- Work with QA/Testing to support testability and agree on technical quality expectations
- Consult the Product Manager when technical trade-offs affect product scope or outcomes
- Coordinate with Security/Privacy Leads on relevant design risks and controls

---

## Release and Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release and Operations Leads coordinate operational readiness and the handoff of changes into production.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment readiness, operational handoff, and release procedures
- Confirm monitoring and support needs are understood
- Prepare or review rollback and mitigation plans

### Goals
- Make releases predictable and operationally supportable
- Reduce production risk through clear readiness and recovery plans

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to confirm release evidence and prepare smoke tests
- Coordinate release timing, readiness, and stakeholder updates with the Project Manager
- Partner with the Technical Lead on operational requirements and with the Security/Privacy Lead on applicable release controls
- Share operational feedback and production outcomes with the Product Manager and delivery team

---

## Security and Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads advise teams on security and privacy risks, requirements, and controls relevant to the initiative.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security and privacy requirements and risks
- Advise on controls and review relevant designs or implementation plans
- Help coordinate escalation of security or privacy concerns through established processes

### Goals
- Make security and privacy responsibilities visible throughout delivery
- Help the team address relevant risks before release

### Typical Communication and Interactions
- Collaborate with Developers and QA/Testing on implementation and verification of applicable controls
- Work with the Technical Lead on design risks and with the Release and Operations Lead on production readiness
- Coordinate with the Project Manager to track security/privacy risks, dependencies, and escalations
- Advise the Product Manager on how relevant risks or requirements may affect scope and outcomes

---

## Combining Roles
- On smaller projects, one person may take on more than one role.
- Keep responsibilities and decision ownership explicit even when roles are combined.

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
