# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. Teams first align on the problem, stakeholders, goals, and success measures, then shape approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, milestones, dependencies, and a Definition of Done. Delivery proceeds iteratively with regular team check-ins and reviews, risk tracking, and appropriate testing. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, communication, and escalations while Product leads outcomes and prioritization. Teams verify release readiness, then review results, capture learnings, and track improvement actions. Customer value, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety guide the process.
